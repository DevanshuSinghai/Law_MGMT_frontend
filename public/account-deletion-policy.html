Account Deletion Policy

LawyerFlow – Legal Practice Management App

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

LawyerFlow respects your right to control your personal data. This policy explains how to delete your account, what data gets removed, and what data may be retained after deletion.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Export or back up any case files, documents, or data you need before requesting deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

You can Delete Your Account By Sending us Email

Send a deletion request to legalpapersanalytics@gmail.com from your registered email address with subject: "Account Deletion Request – LawyerFlow"

Deleting Specific Data Without Closing Your Account

You do not have to close your LawyerFlow account to remove data. Signed in to the app or the web dashboard, you can delete individual clients, cases, documents and tasks at any time. Deleting a record removes it and any files uploaded against it from our systems; copies held in backups are purged within 90 days.

Or Ask Us to Remove It For You

If you would rather we did it, or you want a whole category of records removed at once, email legalpapersanalytics@gmail.com from your registered email address with subject: "Data Deletion Request – LawyerFlow", telling us what you would like removed. We action these within 30 days.

Data Type Removed on Request Notes
Clients, cases, documents, tasks Yes Also deletable by you in the app at any time
Client identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN and similar) Yes Can be cleared without deleting the client record
Profile photo and contact details Yes Your account stays open
Anonymised activity logs Retained Up to 1 year, for security purposes
Records shared at firm level Retained Remain available to your firm until the firm account is deleted

Your account, login and firm membership are unaffected by any of the above. To remove the account itself, follow the steps above.

Deletion Timeline

Stage Timeframe
Request acknowledged Within 3 business days
Account deactivated (login blocked) Within 7 days of request
Personal data permanently deleted from servers Within 30 days of request
Data removed from backups Within 90 days

What Gets Deleted

Data Type Status After Deletion
Your personal account (name, email, password) Permanently Deleted
Your personal profile and preferences Permanently Deleted
Cases and clients you created (if you are the sole firm member) Permanently Deleted
Documents you uploaded (if you are the sole firm member) Permanently Deleted
Your case notes (private notes) Permanently Deleted
Tasks assigned to you personally Permanently Deleted

What May Be Retained

Some data may be retained where legally required or where it is shared firm-level data that belongs to the firm, not your individual account.

Data Type Reason for Retention Retention Period
Cases, clients, and documents shared within an active firm Firm data is owned by the firm, not the individual user. Other firm members' work cannot be disrupted. Until firm account is deleted
Case assignments showing your role on a case Firm audit trail integrity Until firm account is deleted
Anonymized activity logs Security and fraud prevention Up to 1 year
Data required by applicable law Legal obligation As required by law

Where data is retained for firm continuity, your personal identity (name, email) is disassociated from those records.

Firm Administrator Deletion

If you are the sole administrator of a law firm account in LawyerFlow:

  • All firm data (cases, clients, documents, tasks) will also be permanently deleted
  • If other members exist under the firm, you must either transfer admin rights or confirm you wish to delete the entire firm workspace
  • Deletion of a firm workspace will remove all associated users' firm-linked data

Caution: Deleting a firm workspace affects all firm members. As admin, notify your team before proceeding.

Cancellation Before Deletion Completes

You may cancel a pending deletion request within 7 days of submission by emailing legalpapersanalytics@gmail.com. After account deactivation is complete, cancellation is no longer possible.
